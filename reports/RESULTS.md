## 8. Limitations (honest)
- **Small, single-orchard dataset** (~5k, one farm in Vinh Long) → limited
  background/cultivar/lighting diversity; high artifact-shortcut risk.
- **Class confusability** is biological (Sooty mold follows Mealybug; multiple
  *Phytophthora* diseases) — some confusion is intrinsic, not just model error.
- **OOD fragility:** web/social images differ in capture, framing, and may be
  mislabelled; expect a real ID→OOD drop.
- **Softmax-threshold OOD** can still be confidently wrong on far-OOD inputs;
  energy/Mahalanobis are stronger but deferred ([DL-THRESH]).
- **Single held-out split** (no k-fold) for timeline reasons → wider CIs.
- **No GPU/data in the generation sandbox** → numbers here must come from your run.
